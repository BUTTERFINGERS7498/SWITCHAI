Who did what

Every change, in plain words. humanagenthospital system

    For people like Margaret

    About 13,000 people a day in England are well enough to leave hospital but are still in a bed, around 1 in 10 beds. For an 82-year-old, an afternoon in a ward is not a neutral wait.

    For families like Sarah's

    They wait for a time nobody can give. Homeward keeps one honest answer for each person: what they're waiting on, who owns it, and when.

    For the nurses

    The chasing moves to agents. Every outward action still needs a nurse's yes, and agents refuse clinical work: fitness, medicines and letters stay with clinicians.

    Figures: NHS England delayed-discharge statistics as summarised in the project's research dossier (£562 per bed-day). Everyone on this board is invented.
